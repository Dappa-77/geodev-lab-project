# My project brief

## The question
Which settlements in Obio-Akpor Local Government Area have no paved road access within 2 km of a health facility?

## Why it matters
Road access shapes how quickly people can reach a clinic, especially during emergencies or the rainy season when unpaved roads can become impassable. Mapping this gap would help identify settlements that look "covered" on a straight-line map but are actually hard to reach, which is relevant to my background in geotechnical and coastal engineering and to infrastructure planning in the Niger Delta.

## The data I need
- Administrative/LGA boundary — DIVA-GIS — http://www.diva-gis.org/gdata — Shapefile — a few MB
- Roads — GRID3 NGA Roads v1.0 — https://data.grid3.org/datasets/11f0f007bf68404dab4ecb96a535e52b_0/explore — GeoPackage/Shapefile — size shown at download
- Health facilities — GRID3 NGA Health Facilities v2.0 — https://data.grid3.org/datasets/a0ed9627a8b240ff8b315a84575754a4_0/explore — GeoPackage/Shapefile — size shown at download

## What I would build
A map showing which settlements in Obio-Akpor LGA fall outside a 2 km buffer of the road-accessible route to the nearest health facility. Eventually, a tool an LGA health officer could open to see access gaps at a glance, and re-run as new roads or facilities are added.
